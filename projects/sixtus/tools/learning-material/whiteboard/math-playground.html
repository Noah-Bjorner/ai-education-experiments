Supported syntax and font coverage

Greek letters, fractions, roots, scripts, sums, integrals, matrices, cases, and aligned equations use MathJax base and AMS math. You can include one surrounding pair of $…$, $$…$$, \(…\), or \[…\].

This accepts math expressions, not full LaTeX documents or package imports. Missing symbols and unsupported commands show an error. The font uses Medium for ordinary bold and italic styles; calligraphic and Fraktur alphabets are unavailable. Double-struck C, N, P, Q, R, and Z are supported.

Everything renders locally. No AI calls, uploads, or external fonts. Increase the board dimensions if a long expression will not fit legibly.
